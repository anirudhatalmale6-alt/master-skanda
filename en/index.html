The practice

Master Skanda receives clients at 1485 MacDonald Street in Saint-Laurent, near Côte-Vertu metro station.

Consultations deal with the decisions people hesitate to take alone: a professional direction, growth to prepare for, a financial question that keeps coming back, a partnership to weigh up.

The framework is set in advance: a confidential conversation, a spiritual and astrological perspective, no promised outcome, and nothing that commits you beyond the meeting itself.

  • Confidential

    What is said during a consultation stays in the room. This site never asks you to write your situation down.

  • No promises

    No guaranteed outcome, no assured return, no pressure. A consultation is a perspective, not a solution.

  • In person, near the metro

    The practice is in Saint-Laurent, near Côte-Vertu metro station. The exact address and directions are on the Contact page.

Consultations

Four consultations are offered at launch. They follow the subjects the owner's recent communication covers: business matters, growth and career, financial concerns and partnerships.

  • Business matters

    A consultation to lay out a business decision and look at what actually weighs on it.

  • Growth and career

    A consultation around a professional transition, a step forward, or a change of direction.

  • Financial concerns

    An astrological reflection around a financial concern. This is not financial advice and cannot take its place.

  • Partnerships

    A consultation on the dynamics of a business partnership: what fits, what rubs, what has not been said.

The catalogue remains provisional until the owner has settled the exact list, durations, formats and prices.

The framework

The framework is stated up front, not afterwards. The Approach and ethics page sets out what a consultation is, what it is not, and what is never asked of you.

Approach and ethics

Consultations offer a spiritual or astrological perspective for personal reflection. Results cannot be guaranteed. They do not replace the services of a health, legal, financial or public safety professional. In an emergency, contact the appropriate services.

Adults only.
