Privacy policy

What this site collects, what it does not have, and what remains to be decided.

This page is a working draft. The specification says so itself: legal requirements must be validated by a qualified professional before launch. This text is not legal advice and does not replace that validation.

What is true today

  • The site sets no cookies

    No analytics cookie, no advertising cookie, no session cookie. That is why there is no consent banner: there would be nothing to consent to.

  • No request leaves the domain

    Fonts, images and scripts are served from the site itself. No map, no video and no social button is loaded from a third party.

  • No analytics tool is installed

    No Google Analytics, no equivalent. If measurement is added later, it will capture neither the free text of messages nor any confidential information — that is section 8.2 of the specification.

  • The form does not send anything yet

    It validates in the browser and stops there. No data is transmitted, stored or retained at this stage.

  • External links are links, not loads

    Opening the listing or the directions takes you to another site, where other rules apply. Nothing leaves until you click.
