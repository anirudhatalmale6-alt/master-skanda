Business matters

A consultation to lay out a business decision and look at what actually weighs on it.

When people come

  • A decision that has dragged on for weeks without being made.
  • A choice between two directions that seem equally defensible.
  • The need to talk a professional matter over with someone outside the business.

What a consultation can offer

  • Time to step back, away from the usual noise of the decision.
  • A symbolic reading of the situation, put in plain words.
  • Questions no one else has asked you.

What it does not replace

  • Legal, accounting or tax advice.
  • A market study or a business plan.
  • Any guarantee about how the decision turns out.
